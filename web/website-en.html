GLOBAL B2B · PHYSICAL GOODS ONLY

Source-backed trade opportunities, without invented facts.

Global Broker is being built to research international physical-goods procurement opportunities, analyze tender evidence and prepare supplier-matching workflows while keeping unknown fields explicitly unknown.

Pre-launch / Shadow Mode: no automatic bids, emails, orders or payments are sent.

PLATFORM

From public notice to evidence-led review

1. Discover

Official and public procurement sources are scanned for physical-goods opportunities.

2. Verify

Country, buyer, quantity, deadlines, specifications and documents are tracked with provenance.

3. Prepare

Supplier research, feasibility and offer preparation stay blocked until required evidence exists.

A listed opportunity is not a guaranteed transaction, verified supplier mandate, export approval or profit claim.
MARKETS

Europe, Gulf markets and global procurement sources

Gulf source links are currently registered for research, but they are not described as live feeds until real notice ingestion, document provenance and refresh tests pass.
